# Factory pack-and-close verification protocol (to be executed by Operations; nothing here has been performed)

Gate G13 stays **UNVERIFIED** until a signed copy of this protocol is returned with photographs. Measurements in millimetres to 0.5 mm; photograph every step with a ruler or tape in frame; record the date, inspector and carton batch.

## Common preparation
1. Use production cartons (not samples) and production primary packs filled to normal weight. Record batch ids.
2. Measure and record the **internal** length, breadth and height of three cartons (flaps open, from inner wall to inner wall; height from the inner floor to the top edge of the side walls).
3. Photograph the printed carton-size label.

## 666×6 — six Deluxe 666 boxes (internal 393.7 × 273.05 × 349.25 mm; confirm by measurement)
- Measure three closed boxes: length, breadth, height **with** lid and film. Record whether 115 mm includes the lid.
- Pack in the specified arrangement: two across the length, one deep, three levels, tray order bottom-left, bottom-right, middle-left, middle-right, top-left, top-right.
- Record clearance at the tightest axis (calculated height margin 4.25 mm; length 13.7 mm; breadth 8.05 mm).
- Close all four flaps; apply tape along the long seam. Pass: flaps meet without force, no box deformation or lid lift, no visible crushing, carton does not bulge more than 3 mm on any face (owner to confirm tolerance).

## 888×4 — four 888 boxes (internal 527.05 × 349.25 × 336.55 mm; **printed label says 20 in length**)
- Measure the internal length of three cartons and **reconcile it with 20.75 in (527.05 mm)** and with the printed 20 in; record which is right.
- Measure three closed boxes (340 × 260 × 160 mm listing values) with lid.
- Pack two across, one deep, two levels. Calculated length margin 7.05 mm (tightest), breadth 9.25 mm, height 16.55 mm.
- Close and tape as above; same pass criteria.

## RTC1×10 — ten 1 kg trays (internal 330.2 × 215.9 × 330.2 mm)
- Measure ten closed tray-and-lid units: individual height, then stacked height of ten (note any nesting). The calculated stack is 320 mm for ten nominal 32 mm units; lids may add height.
- Check footprint fit (calculated breadth margin 5.9 mm, length 30.2 mm), lid interference, flap clearance and closure.
- The **40 mm** option is a separate configuration: if it is to be sold, repeat with 40 mm trays and record the achievable count; do not reuse the ten-tray result.

## External stacking (all three)
Record the four-closed-carton stacking evidence the owner relies on (supplier compression/box-compression certificate, or a documented stack test). **Dimensions do not prove load-bearing safety.** Attach the certificate or test record.
